Our Mission

What is Chotelal Clinics?

Chotelal Clinics was created with a clear objective: "Making health information easier to understand and access through AI."

Chotelal Ji serves as an intuitive digital companion that translates medical jargon into clear Hindi and English explanations, helping individuals make informed lifestyle choices before seeing their physician.

⚠️ Educational AI • Not a Certified Medical Practitioner
Instant Symptom Clarity
Discuss symptoms and receive structured general wellness advice within seconds.
Hindi-First Voice Experience
Speak naturally in Hindi with browser speech recognition for hands-free queries.
Privacy Minded & Zero Key Leaks
Operates cleanly on static hosting without demanding sensitive patient data.
⚠️
Official Medical & Emergency Safety Notice

Chotelal Ji is an artificial intelligence health assistant designed for informational and educational purposes only. It is NOT a replacement for a qualified doctor, clinical diagnosis, or emergency medical service. If you are experiencing sudden, severe, or life-threatening symptoms (such as chest pressure, acute difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, or loss of consciousness), please contact local emergency services (112 / 108 / 911) or visit the nearest hospital emergency room immediately.
